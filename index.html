I am a Ph.D. student in Control Science and Engineering at Beijing Jiaotong University. My research connects brain–computer interfaces, artificial intelligence, and transportation to better understand drivers and human–vehicle interaction.

I work with multimodal physiological signals, including EEG, to study driver states, driving behavior, and human acceptance of automated vehicles.
