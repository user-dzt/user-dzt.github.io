Education: I am a Ph.D. student in Control Science and Engineering in the integrated Master–Ph.D. program at Beijing Jiaotong University (BJTU). I am currently a visiting Ph.D. student at the University of Birmingham, United Kingdom, on a one-year joint-training program supported by the China Scholarship Council (CSC). I received my Bachelor's degree in Transportation Engineering from Chang'an University in 2023.

Research Interests: Brain–computer interfaces and intelligent transportation, including:
● Brain–Computer Interfaces
● Human–Vehicle Interaction
● AI in Transportation
● Automated Vehicles

My research uses EEG and multimodal physiological signals to investigate driver states, driving behavior, and human acceptance of automated vehicles. For research discussions and collaboration, please contact ztdong@bjtu.edu.cn or zhentao.dong@outlook.com.
